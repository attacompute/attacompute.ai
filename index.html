Common questions

Plain answers on USDC billing, Phala TEE verification, and request privacy.

How do USDC inference prices work?

Fund your AntSeed wallet with USDC on Base or card checkout in Desktop. Usage is metered per request with zero subscription, and free models work with $0.

Why are two attacompute seller addresses listed?

Our original peer ran from May 2026; on Nov 18, 2026 active inference moved to our Phala TEE peer. Both histories are stitched in the chart above.

Are AntSeedStats USDC totals operator profit?

No. Displayed USDC figures are gross on-chain settled volume before protocol fees, reported directly by AntSeedStats.

Which OpenAI and Anthropic API clients work?

Any OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible client works against your local buyer (:8377) or local gateway (:8379).

How does request privacy work?

Private mode enforces Phala TEE attestation. Data needed to fulfill your request is processed by attacompute and the service providers involved; do not send sensitive info you do not want processed.
