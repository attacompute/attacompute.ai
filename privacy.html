Privacy policy

Plain summary of how inference requests and this public website handle data.

Inference requests on AntSeed

Data needed to fulfill an inference request is processed by attacompute and the service providers involved in generating the response. Do not send sensitive, confidential, or regulated information you do not want processed.

Requests routed through AntSeed do not require an account, name, or email address on attacompute. Payment settlement occurs on-chain in USDC via the AntSeed protocol.

This website

This static website sets no tracking cookies and loads no third-party font CDNs or ad trackers. Your browser fetches public seller statistics directly from antseedstats.com and caches the latest public JSON response in localStorage to display stats quickly if the API is temporarily unreachable.

Independent operator

attacompute is an independent inference provider operated from Texas and is not affiliated with the AntSeed Foundation.
